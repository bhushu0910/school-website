About Our School

Discover what makes St. Ann's English Medium High School & Nandura a premier educational institution

VISION

To create a new generation of integrally wise, innovatively intelligent, emotionally balanced, culturally rich, honestly altruistic, socially effective, morally sound, and spiritually oriented pupils.

MISSION

  • To provide a learning environment conducive to academic excellence where the spiritual, moral, intellectual, emotional, physical, cultural, and social needs of each student can be met.
  • To adopt and embrace the principles of spirituality, sharing and service.
  • To acquire a clear set of values that will guide their actions, choices and decisions throughout life.
  • To foster creativity, critical thinking and problem solving for personal growth.
  • To promote peace, justice, and concern for those less fortunate.
  • To teach acceptance, tolerance, and respect for all.

Our Key Features

What sets St. Ann's apart from other educational institutions

Modern Laboratories

State-of-the-art science and computer labs equipped with latest technology for practical learning experiences.

Well-Stocked Library

Extensive collection of books, journals, and digital resources to encourage reading and research.

Sports Facilities

Spacious playgrounds and facilities for cricket, football, basketball, and indoor sports.

Arts & Culture

Music, dance, drama, and art programs to nurture creativity and cultural appreciation.

Safe Environment

Transportation

Safe and reliable school bus service covering all major areas of the city.

Our Academic Programs

Comprehensive educational programs tailored for different age groups

Primary Education (Grades 1-4)

Our primary program focuses on building strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, and social skills through interactive and play-based learning methods.

English, Marathi, Hindi, Mathematics
Environmental Studies
Art, Music, Physical Education
Value Education

Middle School (Grades 5-7)

Our middle school program encourages independent thinking and exploration across various subjects while developing critical thinking skills.

English, Marathi, Hindi, Mathematics
Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
Social Studies (History, Geography)
Computer Science

High School (Grades 8-10)

Our high school program prepares students for board examinations while offering elective subjects to explore individual interests and career paths.

English, Marathi, Hindi, Mathematics
Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
Social Studies (History, Geography, Civics)
Computer Applications
